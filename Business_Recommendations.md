# Business Recommendations

## Key Findings

- Annual members completed significantly more rides than casual riders.
- Casual riders had longer average ride durations.
- Ride activity peaked during the middle and end of the work week.
- Electric bikes were the most frequently used rideable type.

## Recommendations

1. Encourage casual riders to become annual members by promoting membership benefits.
2. Target marketing campaigns toward casual riders with longer rides.
3. Increase promotions during peak riding days.
4. Continue expanding electric bike availability to meet customer demand.
